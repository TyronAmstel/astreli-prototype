# ASTRELI Brand Direction

## Core idea

**ASTRELI is the AI female model and digital muse who showcases the jewellery and each collection.**

She should be recognisable as the same woman across the homepage, campaigns, collection imagery, and every product page. Her styling and setting may change to express a collection, but her face, age, proportions, and overall identity should remain consistent.

## Product proposition

**Lab diamonds in silver.**

ASTRELI creates genuine lab-grown diamond jewellery primarily in 925 sterling silver. Selective gold plating may be used when it serves the design, but sterling silver remains the material foundation and price-point anchor.

## Collection philosophy

**Collections. Curated, never crowded.**

Collections should be small, intentional, and clearly edited. The experience must feel considered and collectible—not like an endless stream of disposable products. Every piece should communicate permanence, emotional value, and a reason to remain in someone’s jewellery wardrobe.

## Visual guardrails

- Use one consistent ASTRELI muse across all model photography.
- Keep jewellery at realistic, wearable scale and match the exact product being sold.
- Prioritise polished silver, restrained gold plating, natural skin, and refined styling.
- Let each collection have its own mood without changing ASTRELI’s identity.
- Avoid generic stock imagery, bridal clichés, fast-fashion styling, oversized stones, and visual clutter.
- Use the label **WORN BY ASTRELI** only when the image follows this identity system.

## Brand shorthand

> ASTRELI is an AI muse presenting curated lab-grown diamond jewellery in sterling silver—modern pieces designed to be kept.
